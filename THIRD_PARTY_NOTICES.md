## Common Weakness Enumeration (CWE)

Antares CLI bundles a derived snapshot of the Common Weakness Enumeration
(CWE) taxonomy for CWE selection and finding metadata. The source material is
published by The MITRE Corporation at <https://cwe.mitre.org/>. The same notice
is shipped beside the derived data in the Python package.
